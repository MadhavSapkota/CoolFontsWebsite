Why a keyboard beats copy and paste

Generator websites are handy once. They become tiring when you want styled text in everyday messages: leave the chat, open a browser, type, choose a style, copy, go back and paste. A keyboard removes those steps because the styled text starts where you are already typing.

Check privacy before installing any keyboard

A keyboard handles what you type, so privacy should be part of any comparison. Android shows a warning when you enable a third-party keyboard, and it is worth taking seriously for every app. Cool Fonts Keyboard does not send what you type, or usage data, off your phone. You can read the details in our privacy policy. For any other keyboard, read its privacy policy and Data safety section on Google Play.

What fancy keyboard fonts can and cannot do

Good expectations matter. Styled fonts are special Unicode characters, so they change the text you type, not your phone's system font. Most modern devices show common styles, but rarer characters may appear as boxes on some phones, and some name fields do not accept them. Screen readers may also read styled letters badly, so keep important details in plain text.

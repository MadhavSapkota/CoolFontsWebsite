Kaomoji FAQ

How do I type the kaomoji shrug?

The shrug is ¯\_(ツ)_/¯. Most keyboards can’t type ¯ or ツ, so tap it above to copy, or use the Kaomoji tab in Cool Fonts Keyboard to insert it in one tap.

Are kaomoji and Japanese emoticons the same?

Yes. Kaomoji is the Japanese word for these text faces, which English speakers also call Japanese emoticons, kawaii faces or text faces.

What does kaomoji mean?

Kaomoji comes from Japanese: kao (face) + moji (character). They became popular on Japanese internet forums in the 1980s and 90s.

What's the difference between kaomoji and emoji?

Emoji are single pictures drawn by your phone. Kaomoji are made from several text characters, so they look the same style as your text and can be customized endlessly.

Why does my kaomoji show as boxes?

The viewer's phone may be missing a symbol used in it. Use common kaomoji like (◕‿◕) or ʕ•ᴥ•ʔ, or preview it live with a kaomoji keyboard before sending.

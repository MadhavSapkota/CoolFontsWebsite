Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

This policy covers the Cool Fonts Keyboard Android app and the website at coolfontskeyboard.app.

The Android keyboard

Cool Fonts Keyboard does not send what you type off your phone. The app has no accounts.

The app uses Google Firebase for analytics and advertising. These services may collect app usage events, device information and identifiers such as an app instance ID or Android advertising ID. Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Android may show a general warning when you enable any third-party keyboard. Google Play handles app downloads and updates under Google's own privacy policy.

The website

  • Saved on your device: the site uses your browser's local storage to remember preferences and items you save. This stays in your browser and is not sent to us. You can clear it in your browser settings.
  • Clipboard: the site writes to your clipboard only when you tap to copy something.
  • Service providers: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers receive standard request information, such as your IP address, when your browser loads the site, and handle it under their own privacy policies.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics runs only if you choose Accept, and then counts page visits and Play Store link clicks. Our analytics property is linked to Google Ads, so Google may also use this information for ad measurement. On this site we turn off advertising cookies and personalized advertising signals. If you do not accept, it is not loaded. Text you type into our generators stays in your browser and is never sent to us or to Google. Change your analytics choice

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
